## Resources

Here are some useful resources for NU students:

> [Registrar](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz)

You may already be familiar with it, as it is the platform on which you register courses, but that website has some other useful features as well. Those include:  
- [Course catalog](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz/course-catalog)  
    Here you can search for courses, but do mind the dates, some of them are deprecated

- [Course schedule](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz/course-schedules)

- [Course list with requirements](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz/course-schedules)  
    Here you can see all the courses taught in a given semester, together with all the pre-, co-, and anti-requisites.

> [Academic Advising Office](https://aao.nu.edu.kz/)

There you can find your Academic Handbook(important) and your advisor's email (not important).

> [Strategy and Delivery Office](https://ie.nu.edu.kz/)

Up until writing this guide, I did not even know how this office is called. The only useful feature there is course statistics.

> [Nuspace](https://nuspace.kz) (**Not affiliated with NU**)

Just a student-made open-source superapp. You can find course statistics, plan course registration, calculate & project GPA, create and see student-driven events, etc. Remember to register using your NU email, otherwise the registrarion will fail.


## Special Courses

Our department offers several special math courses, those include:

<a id="special-topics"></a>
> MATH 490 Special Topics in Mathematics I  
> MATH 491 Special Topics in Mathematics II  
> MATH 492 Special Topics in Mathematics III

These three courses are simply a way for undergraduate students to take graduate-level courses. To open one just email/have a talk with the teaching professor. As long as they are willing - *and they usually are* - those courses will get opened. They are registered using **Add Course Form** in [Student Request](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz/my-registrar/student-requests) of Registrar.

<a id="directed-study"></a>
> MATH 497 Directed Study in Mathematics I  
> MATH 498 Directed Study in Mathematics II

The purpose of these courses is two-fold. One is in the name: it is a directed self-study course, usually for a Capstone Project topic. Another purpose is to serve as a continuation of [Special Topics](#special-topics) Courses. After completing all 3 [Special Topics](#special-topics) Courses, it's impossible to take any of them again, so if you wish to register it, you need to register the corresponding Directed Studies section. The registration is usually done via **Add Course Form**, similarly to [Special Topics](#special-topics) courses, however sometimes it can be registered as a regular course.


> MATH 499 Capstone Project

Capstone Project can only registered in Spring semester of the 4th year of study. This is not a regular course, but rather a research project made under the supervision of the faculty member. Though it can only be registered during the Spring semester, the actual work can be started before(both "unofficially" and "officially" by registering [Directed Studies](#directed-study) course), just discuss it with your supervisor-to-be.
